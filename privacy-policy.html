Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TeamoTrack handles information when organisations and their authorised users use our workforce-management services and website.

Effective date: 15 October 2026

1. Who operates TeamoTrack?

TeamoTrack is operated by MD KASHIF NEZAM (“we”, “us”, or “our”). For privacy questions, email contact@teamotrack.com.

2. Information that may be collected

  • Account and organisation information: name, work email, phone number, role, organisation and team details, and account identifiers.
  • Workforce records: attendance entries, check-in/check-out times, shifts, breaks, leave, holidays, tasks, regularisation requests and related approvals.
  • Location information: GPS or location data when a feature is enabled and the relevant device permissions are granted. Organisation settings and device permissions may affect availability.
  • Images: selfie or other images submitted for attendance verification where that feature is enabled.
  • Payment and support information: plan, transaction/order references, billing status and messages sent to support. Payment card or bank details are generally handled by the payment provider rather than stored by TeamoTrack.
  • Technical information: device, browser, diagnostic, security and usage information needed to operate and protect the service.

3. How information is used

We use information to provide and maintain the service; manage attendance and workforce workflows; support location-based or selfie-based features chosen by an organisation; process payments and subscriptions; respond to support requests; protect accounts; troubleshoot issues; and meet legal obligations.

4. Organisation administrators and employee data

When an employer or organisation uses TeamoTrack, its authorised administrators may access workforce information according to their roles and the organisation’s configuration. The organisation is responsible for providing its workers with appropriate notices and ensuring that its use of employee, location and image data has a valid legal basis. Users should contact their organisation administrator for questions about workplace monitoring settings.

5. Service providers and sharing

We may use service providers that help operate the service, such as cloud hosting, database, authentication, analytics, communications and payment providers. Information may be shared with those providers only as needed for the relevant service, security, support or legal compliance. We do not sell personal information for money.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a lawful business transfer. We do not control the separate privacy practices of third-party websites or providers.

6. Storage, security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, support the organisation’s records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and enforce agreements. Retention may vary by data type and organisation settings.

7. Your choices and requests

You can manage device permissions, including location and camera permissions, through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information where applicable. If information is controlled by your employer or organisation, we may refer the request to its administrator.

8. Children

TeamoTrack is a workforce-management service and is not intended for children to use independently.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
